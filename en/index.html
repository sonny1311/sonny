Persistent browser strategy and management games

Nadena Games develops browser games built around long-term decisions rather than short sessions. Each game focuses on a different management fantasy: running a farm, building a company, managing a football club or developing colonies in space.

The current game interfaces are primarily German. These English pages provide an international overview while localization is expanded.

Hofhain

A long-term browser farming and management game. Grow crops, raise animals, plan storage and production, trade goods, complete orders and expand your farm step by step.

Built for players who like systems that connect

The four games use different themes, but they share one design principle: progress should come from understanding how systems influence each other. A larger farm needs storage and supply. A larger company needs purchasing, staff, machines and logistics. A stronger football club needs both sporting and financial planning. A growing space society needs energy, resources, research and transport to expand together.

That makes the games suitable for players who enjoy planning over time, checking the effect of earlier decisions and gradually opening new parts of a larger system. Nadena Games is browser-first, so players can access the worlds directly without a traditional game installation. Each title remains its own game with its own rules, progression and identity.
