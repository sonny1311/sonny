Nadena Games – official press kit

Nadena Games develops persistent browser strategy and management games with a focus on long-term progression, interconnected systems and player-driven development. The current portfolio covers farming, business simulation, football management and space strategy.

Games

Hofhain

Genre: farming simulation / management / economy

Hofhain is a persistent browser farming and management game. Players grow crops, raise animals, manage storage and production, trade goods, complete orders and expand a farm into a larger interconnected operation.

ORVUNO

Genre: business simulation / economy / management

ORVUNO is a persistent browser business simulation where purchasing, deliveries, storage, staff, machinery, production, customer demand and logistics form one connected operational chain.

English overviewPlay / official site

Artwork: nadena-games.de/assets/orvuno.webp

FUTNARO

Genre: football management / sports management

FUTNARO is an online football manager for the browser. Players build a squad, set line-ups and tactics, train players, manage transfers and youth development, expand infrastructure and compete in league and cup football.

English overviewPlay / official site

Artwork: nadena-games.de/assets/futnaro.webp

AstraWelle

Genre: space strategy / colony management / economy

AstraWelle is a strategic browser management game about colonies, population, resources, energy, production chains, research, trade, logistics, shipbuilding and long-term expansion.
